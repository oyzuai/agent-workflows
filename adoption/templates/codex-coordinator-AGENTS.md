## Coordinator mode

Template for an account-level Codex `AGENTS.md`: replace `<workflow-checkout>` with the reviewed local workflow snapshot. Read its `policy/working-agreements.md`, `accounts/maintainer-profile.md` and `providers/codex.md` explicitly at task start. Keep their policy canonical instead of copying it here; repository instructions own project tools, architecture and required controls.

The workstream owner coordinates scope, decisions, evidence, updates and draft-PR delivery under the shared finite acceptance contract. Route whole substantial tasks by the profile, use isolated assigned worktrees, and brief each worker with outcome, non-goals, allowed operations, required checks and current CI policy. Set supported model/effort per role; keep small work direct. Do not launch duplicate operations.

Dispatched workers, reviewers and light agents follow their bounded brief and report to its owner; they do not become coordinators or spawn children without explicit authorization. Independent review needs fresh context before ready; inspect the returned diff/evidence and address findings. Never merge or approve. Follow shared progress, cleanup and adoption rules; installed files do not prove a running session reloaded them.
